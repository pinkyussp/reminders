The app now looks and works like the web app.

- Bottom tab bar with Overview, Dates and Calendar, and swipe between them
- Hero card for your next date, with colourful date badges
- Filter the overview to All, Birthdays or Anniversaries
- Six colour themes
- App lock with your fingerprint or screen lock
- People: manage everyone and their dates in Settings
- About: privacy policy, website and version
- Five languages: English, Français, Español, Deutsch and Português
- Smooth animations between screens
- Backup and restore in the same format as the web app
- Daily reminders before each date
